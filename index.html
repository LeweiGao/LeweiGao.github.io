I use spatial data, maps and urban research to explore how cities are built, accessed and experienced.

My work sits between GIS and urban planning, with a focus on clear spatial analysis and visual communication. I hold a Master of Urban Innovation from the University of Toronto and a background in GIS and Human Geography.
